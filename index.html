MethodMatched properties across 2022 to 2024, then used t-tests, ANOVA with Tukey HSD and OLS; results are associations, not causal claims.

I am drawn to problems that begin vaguely: a market is shifting, two metrics disagree or a recurring report takes too long to trust. My first move is to clarify who needs to decide what, then work backward to the evidence.

Studying Computing Science and Business taught me to care about both sides of the handoff. Analysis is only finished when someone can use it, explain it and return to it next quarter without starting over.
