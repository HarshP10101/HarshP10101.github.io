04 Retail analytics / machine learning

Market Basket Analysis

Which items tend to share a basket?

2,363whole-milk occurrences

Tools & sources

Python · pandas · MLxtend · Apriori · K-Means

View project on GitHub ↗

01 / Context

Why it mattered

Product popularity gives retailers a starting point. The items that people buy together can suggest a different set of ideas for store layout, bundles and promotions.

02 / Method

How I approached it

I removed duplicate member-date-item rows, then used Apriori association rules to explore co-purchases. I used K-Means to explore customer segments and compared pricing scenarios in the notebook.

03 / Findings

What came out of it

Whole milk appeared most often in the cleaned records, with 2,363 occurrences. The association study highlighted combinations involving staples such as whole milk, yogurt and rolls or buns as candidates for testing.

04 / Takeaway

What I learned

Frequency and association answer different questions. The pricing and segmentation exercises were exploratory, so their value is in shaping hypotheses for a real test.
